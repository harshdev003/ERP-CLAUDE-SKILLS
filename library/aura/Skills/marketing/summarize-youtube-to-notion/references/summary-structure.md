# Summary structure — worked example

This reference shows the exact structure Claude should produce in the Notion
page body. The full worked example is the Speak / Connor Zwick page filed at
[notion.so/aurahealth/…34c75e23af208142a9e0d7edb3ead3ca](https://www.notion.so/34c75e23af208142a9e0d7edb3ead3ca)
— that's the canonical reference if you want to see a complete output.

The template below is a condensed skeleton. Fill in sections with the
content from the video's transcript, following the style guide in
`SKILL.md` → Step 4.

---

## Template

```markdown
> **Channel:** <channel name> (host: <host name>) — **Guest:** <guest name>, <title>
> **Duration:** <MM:SS> — **Transcript:** <auto-generated | creator-uploaded> <language> captions

## Overview

<3–5 sentences. What the video is, who's in it, and why it's worth the reader's time.
State the kind of value it delivers — strategy learnings, technical deep-dive,
founder story — so the reader can decide whether to keep reading.>

## The Founding Story

<Optional. Include when the video spends meaningful time on how a person or
company got started, and that narrative carries learnings. 3–5 compressed
paragraphs with at least one direct quote embedded. End with the pivotal
moment that set up the rest of the episode.>

> "<The most emblematic quote from the origin story>" — <Speaker> [<MM:SS>]

<Closing paragraph of the story — the "and that led to…" moment.>

## Detailed Learnings

### 1. <Declarative insight statement, not a topic label>

<2–4 paragraphs explaining the insight with concrete examples, numbers, and
context from the video. Don't hedge. State the insight directly.>

> "<A direct quote that captures or evidences the insight>" — <Speaker> [<MM:SS>]

<Optionally a second paragraph that adds nuance, counter-example, or context.>

**<Bolded implication / "so what">:** <one-sentence takeaway that tells the
reader what to do with this insight.>

### 2. <Next declarative insight>

<Same structure — paragraphs, quote, implication.>

> "<quote>" — <Speaker> [<MM:SS>]

**<bolded implication>:** <takeaway.>

<Continue with 5–10 total learnings, clustered by theme, not chronology.
Merge related points the video makes across different timestamps.>

## The Lightning Round

<Optional. Include when the video has an explicit rapid-fire Q&A segment.
Format as question/answer pairs with bolded questions and the guest's actual
words (direct quotes where possible).>

**<Question 1>.** <Guest's answer, with a direct quote if memorable.>

> "<quote>" — <Speaker> [<MM:SS>]

**<Question 2>.** <Guest's answer.>

<Keep it tight — 4–6 Q&As. Cut pleasantries; preserve substance.>

## Closing Thoughts

<Optional. Include when the guest's final answer carries a strong
philosophical, forward-looking, or mission-level take worth preserving
verbatim. 1–2 paragraphs with the final quote embedded.>

> "<final quote>" — <Speaker> [<MM:SS>]

## Takeaways & Action Items for Aura

- **<Bolded lead-in that ties a video learning to an Aura decision>:** <concrete
  action, investigation, or test relevant to Aurie, journaling, sleep coaching,
  growth, org design, or AI product strategy.>
- **<Another lead-in>:** <another action.>
- <4–8 bullets total. This section is what justifies the page existing in the
  Growth Readings database. Always include it.>

## Further Watching

- <Referenced podcast, paper, or creator> — referenced at [<MM:SS>] for <what
  specifically>.
- <Another reference> — at [<MM:SS>].

<Only include items the video *explicitly* references. Skip the section
entirely if the video doesn't point anywhere external.>

---

> 🤖 Generated by `summarize-youtube-to-notion` on YYYY-MM-DD from the
> <auto-generated | creator-uploaded> <language> transcript (<N> segments,
> ~<W> words).
```

## Key stylistic rules (enforce these)

1. **Declarative learning titles.** `### 3. Growth came from technical step-functions, not marketing polish` — not `### 3. Growth`.

2. **Quotes live inside learnings, not in a separate section.** Every learning
   should have at least one direct quote attributed with `— Speaker [MM:SS]`.

3. **Bolded "so what" closes each learning.** `**For AI-native product leaders:**
   the biggest retention wins may be waiting behind the next model capability,
   not the next UX tweak.`

4. **Prose over bullets in narrative sections.** Bullets belong only in
   "Takeaways & Action Items for Aura" and "Further Watching".

5. **Aura takeaways are mandatory, not optional.** Every page needs a
   "Takeaways & Action Items for Aura" section, even if the video has no
   obvious Aura relevance — in that case, make the connection explicit
   (e.g., "worth pressure-testing whether this approach applies to Aurie
   retention").

6. **Cut the uneducational moments.** Sponsor reads, "welcome to the show"
   intros, pleasantries, host repeating back the guest's point — all out.
   Lightning round and stories — in, as long as they carry substance.

7. **Length scales with substance.** 1500–2500 words for a 30–60 minute
   long-form episode. Shorter videos get shorter summaries. Never pad.

8. **Verbatim quotes, corrected prose.** Quoted text is exact from the
   transcript. Your own prose silently corrects auto-caption errors.
